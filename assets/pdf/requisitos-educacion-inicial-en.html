Early Childhood Education

Enrollment requirements

Required age for Early Childhood Education

  • Pre-Jardín: 3 years old by March 31, 2027.
  • Jardín: 4 years old by March 31, 2027.
  • Pre-Escolar: 5 years old by March 31, 2027.

Admissions process

  1. Submit the form. The form must be completed on the website www.colegiocpp.com → Admissions.
  2. Submit all required documents.
  3. Psychological test. An interview date with the psychologist will be set.
  4. Confirmation of admission.
  5. Submit the original documents.
  6. Read and accept the coexistence rules.
  7. Sign the educational services contract.
  8. Payment of the enrollment fee, tuition and the 1st installment of the annual fee.
  9. Final enrollment process.
