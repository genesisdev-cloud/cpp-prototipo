Requirements for all levels

You may submit all documents on paper, or scanned in digital PDF format.

  • Original birth certificate.
  • 2 copies of the student's identity card (both sides).
  • 2 copies of the father's identity card (both sides).
  • 2 copies of the mother's identity card (both sides).
  • 2 passport-size photos of the student.
  • A copy of the previous school report (report card).
  • Proof of payment from the previous school, if it is private.
  • Copy of the vaccination record.
  • Medical examination with an electrocardiogram done in 2026.
  • Endorsed academic record.
